# StrainChain

The [StrainChain public site](https://strainchain.io/) describes cannabis genetics passports built from supplied COAs, with totals recomputed from the source panel.

Its current disclosure labels on-chain strain records and the four-layer audit trail as in development. METRC, BioTrack, and Washington CCRS integrations are roadmap items, not established production integrations.

A supplied COA, QR label, or registry entry does not by itself establish physical authenticity, a laboratory's current licence, product safety, legal sale authority, or regulatory compliance. These require separate, jurisdiction-specific evidence. Do not infer legal seizure or sale instructions from a QR result.

This documentation does not establish customer relationships, automated regulator checks, a universal potency tolerance, rewards, or a public verification API contract.

See [current StrainChain pricing](https://strainchain.io/pricing) for listed offers. This page authorizes no purchase, submission, upload, sharing, webhook, outreach, or blockchain transaction.
