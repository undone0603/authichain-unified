## COA evidence

Record the supplied document's lab name, sample and batch identifiers, issue/test dates, units, test methods, and measured values without changing them. A source document may contain errors; its presence alone is not proof of safety or regulatory compliance.

Current laboratory licensing, accepted test methods, state tracking formats, expiration rules, and potency tolerances require current authoritative sources for the relevant jurisdiction and product. Do not apply a universal numerical threshold, expiry rule, or identifier regex.

Do not use a fixed list of laboratory names as proof of current licensing. Batch reuse or a visual inconsistency is a review observation, not a definitive counterfeit finding. Keep missing or unresolved properties unknown and do not certify legal sale or product safety.
