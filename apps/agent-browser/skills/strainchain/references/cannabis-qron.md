## Supplied cannabis QR evidence

Inventory the supplied QR value, source URL, batch identifier, label, and COA reference. Do not assume a payload schema, reward contract, API endpoint, customer relationship, or brand-specific physical feature from a reference example.

A visually familiar label or hologram does not establish authenticity. A failed scan does not establish counterfeiting. Keep registry evidence, lab evidence, signature verification, state tracking, and physical inspection distinct.

Do not generate legal sale or seizure instructions, send an alert, share a result, claim a reward, or call an undocumented verification endpoint. Report missing evidence as unknown.
