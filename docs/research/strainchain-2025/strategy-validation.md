## 3. Compliance Requirements Analysis

### Legal Framework (2025)
Cannabis NFT projects face complex regulatory landscape:

**SEC Considerations:**
- **Howey Test Risk**: High if NFTs promise investment returns
- **Recent Enforcement**: Impact Theory ($6M fine), Stoner Cats ($1M fine)
- **Safe Approach**: Focus on utility/collectibles, avoid investment language

**Compliance Recommendations:**
1. **Structure as collectibles**, not investments
2. **Avoid revenue-sharing promises**
3. **Implement geographic restrictions**
4. **Age verification (21+) mandatory**
5. **Legal disclaimer on all materials**
6. **Separate legal entity for NFT operations**

### Platform-Specific Compliance
- **Discord**: Educational focus, age-gated channels, no sales facilitation
- **Twitter**: Pre-approval for ads, jurisdiction targeting, no direct sales
- **OpenSea**: Standard marketplace compliance, proper metadata
