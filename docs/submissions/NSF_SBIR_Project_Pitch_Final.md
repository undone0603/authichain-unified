### **1. The Technology Innovation**
AuthiChain is developing the world's first **Hybrid AI-Blockchain Provenance Protocol** designed for high-value industrial and medical assets. Unlike traditional tracking systems that rely on centralized databases (vulnerable to tampering) or simple RFIDs (vulnerable to cloning), AuthiChain utilizes:

*   **Atomic Idempotency:** Our agentic architecture guarantees consistent trust state verification, ensuring auditability and transactional integrity even during high-volume document registration or network instability.
*   **Adversarial Trust Loop:** A dynamic AI consensus mechanism where Security Council agents actively simulate forgery attempts to calibrate trust scores against emerging counterfeit tactics in real-time.
*   **Ed25519-Signed QRONs:** A quantum-resistant cryptographic QR system that embeds digital signatures directly into physical identifiers, providing self-contained proof of origin.

### **2. Technical Challenge & Research Focus**
The primary technical hurdle we are solving is **Disconnected Proof of Origin**. In low-connectivity environments (e.g., medical device storage, border crossings), verification systems often fail. Our NSF-funded research will focus on:

*   **Deterministic Local Verification:** Developing a localized trust-anchor caching system that allows >99% verification accuracy without a network connection.
*   **Bayesian Fraud Detection:** Calibrating our AI agents to mathematically model and predict counterfeit "Scan Storms" before they enter the secondary market.
