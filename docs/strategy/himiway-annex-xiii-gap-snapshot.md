## Why Himiway is on the list

Himiway published a buyer-facing explainer that LMT batteries get a digital battery passport from **18 February 2027**, reachable by QR: https://himiwaybike.com/blogs/news/how-will-the-new-eu-battery-regulation-from-2027

E-bike batteries are Light Means of Transport (LMT) batteries under Regulation (EU) 2023/1542 Article 77.

## Legal hooks (primary text, not a vendor blog)

- Article 77: from 18 February 2027 each LMT battery, each industrial battery > 2 kWh, and each EV battery placed on the market or put into service shall have an electronic record (battery passport).
- The economic operator placing the battery on the market attaches the passport. For batteries made outside the EU that operator is typically the **importer**.
- Annex XIII splits public data from restricted data (performance / state of health is not automatically public to every QR scan).
- Omnibus IV delayed due-diligence duties; it did **not** move the 18 February 2027 passport date.
- Access-rights implementing act was still unfinished as of mid-2026 (Commission pointed to Q4 2026). The passport date itself stayed fixed.

EUR-Lex: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:32023R1542
