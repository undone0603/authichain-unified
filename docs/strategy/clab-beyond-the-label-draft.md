## Beyond the Label: a trust layer for physical goods

A label is a claim. A scanner should be able to tell whether that claim still matches the object in hand.

Counterfeit and diverted goods do not announce themselves. They borrow a brand, a lot number, or a QR that used to mean something. By the time a recall, a lawsuit, or a patient complaint arrives, the original packaging story is already the only record most teams have. That is too late, and it is not evidence.

AuthiChain is a U.S.-based authentication layer for physical goods. A sealed item carries a public verify URL. A phone camera is enough. The scan does not ask the buyer to trust a PDF that can be copied. It checks a signed record: who issued the seal, what chemistry or document was attached, and whether that record has been revoked.

### What a trust layer is not

It is not a marketing site. It is not a promise that a strain tests at 10% of anything. It is not a substitute for a license, a CoA, or counsel.

Where a lab panel prints `<LOQ`, the passport prints an em dash. Missing data stays missing. A ratio is only published when both sides of the pair were measured. That sounds small. It is the difference between a record a lawyer can stand on and a screenshot that collapses under discovery.

### How the stack is supposed to work

1. **Issue.** A farm, brand, or agency attaches a seal to a specific lot or document.
2. **Bind.** The seal points at the source evidence — CoA identifiers, a document hash, a chain-of-custody note — not at a slogan.
3. **Verify.** Anyone with the object scans. The response is authentic, revoked, or not on file. Target time is a couple of seconds.
4. **Keep.** The issuer can export or revoke. AuthiChain does not take an option on the underlying goods or genetics.

QRON is the visible mark (a scannable artwork, not a dead square). StrainChain is the cannabis-facing passport built on the same rule: publish what the panel supports. GovChain is the same idea for credentials and records. One company, one rule: do not invent a number the file does not contain.

### Why operators and counsel should care

Packaging already carries claims. Retail already carries QR codes. The gap is that most of those codes still resolve to a page the brand can edit after the fact. A trust layer that cannot be quietly rewritten is useful in three ordinary fights:

- A buyer asking whether this unit is the unit on the CoA.
- An insurer or opposing counsel asking when the record existed.
- An operator who needs to kill a seal the moment a lot is pulled.

None of that requires a new app install. It requires the issuer to tell the truth about what they measured, and to leave a hole where they did not.

### What AuthiChain will not claim here

We will not quote a finished-flower potency figure we have not reconciled from a named certificate. We will not treat a seed-matrix CoA as a harvest panel. We will not sell a sponsorship as an endorsement of any licensed operator. The article is the product sample: a public, checkable sentence about a physical object.

If a CLAB member wants one cultivar or one SKU mocked as a passport, the onboard path is public. If they want counsel-level language for a specific license, that is a separate conversation with their attorney, not a blog post.
