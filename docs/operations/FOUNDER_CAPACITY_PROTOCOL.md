# Founder capacity protocol

## Purpose

Protect founder decision bandwidth without collecting biometric, sleep, stress,
or other health data in the repository or automation systems.

## Operating rules

1. **One approval digest.** Aggregate owner-gated decisions into the existing
   approval and owner-digest channels; do not create parallel urgent channels
   for routine work.
2. **Work-in-progress cap.** Keep only the highest-priority approval, revenue,
   reliability, and security decisions active. Defer lower-priority work with a
   recorded reason and review date.
3. **Decision packets.** Every escalation must state the decision, evidence,
   impact of waiting, reversible option, and recommended next action.
4. **No inferred urgency.** A workflow may label urgency only from an
   observable deadline, failed control, customer-impacting incident, or owner
   instruction.
5. **Snooze, never silently drop.** Deferred work must have a next review date
   or an explicit retirement decision.

## Automation boundary

Automations may summarize workload, batch non-urgent approvals, and prepare
decision packets. They may not infer founder wellness, access personal health
data, reschedule commitments, or make decisions on the founder's behalf.

## Review

Review deferred work in the owner digest. Any action involving spending,
external contact, legal commitment, production access, or policy changes stays
subject to the operating charter.
