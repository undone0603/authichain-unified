## Phase 2 — Analysis

**Status:** Complete for public first-pass evidence.

### Findings summary

- Scantrust: strongest secure-QR and packaging workflow competitor; third-party pricing signal around $250/month and $650/month for self-serve tiers.
- Authena: strongest feature overlap; blockchain identity, QR/barcode/NFC, DPP, IoT, alerts, analytics; pricing not public.
- Spherity: verifiable identity and DPP compliance; pricing not established publicly.
- Circularise: n-tier supplier data, mass balance/chain of custody, permissioned DPP disclosures; pricing amount not found.
- atma.io/ReadyDPP: connected-product and full-service DPP backed by Avery Dennison; pricing not public.
- MediLedger: pharma-specific permissioned network for contract/chargeback alignment; pricing quote-based.
- VeChain: broad blockchain platform alternative; no comparable SaaS price found.
