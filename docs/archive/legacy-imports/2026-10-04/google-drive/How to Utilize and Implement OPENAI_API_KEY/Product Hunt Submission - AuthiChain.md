## Launch Day Response Templates

### Response to "How is this different from Auth0/Okta?"

Great question! While Auth0/Okta handle authentication, AuthiChain adds blockchain verification and AI fraud detection on top. Think of us as a security layer that makes traditional auth systems tamper-proof and fraud-resistant. We integrate WITH Auth0/Okta, not replace them.

### Response to "What's the blockchain advantage?"

Blockchain provides:

1. **Immutable audit trail** - Every verification is permanently recorded
2. **Decentralization** - No single point of failure
3. **Transparency** - Verifiable by anyone, trusted by everyone
4. **Portability** - NFT credentials work across platforms

Traditional databases can be hacked, modified, or deleted. Blockchain can't.

### Response to "Isn't this overkill for small companies?"

We have a Starter plan at $99/mo for 10K verifications - perfect for startups and SMBs. The ROI comes from:

- Preventing just ONE fraud incident (avg cost: $15K)
- Reducing customer support tickets (40% reduction)
- Faster user onboarding (50% time savings)

Most customers break even in month 1.

### Response to "What about gas fees/blockchain costs?"

We use Polygon (Layer 2) which costs <$0.001 per transaction. All blockchain costs are included in our pricing - no hidden fees or gas charges for your users.

### Response to "How do you ensure privacy/GDPR compliance?"

- Personal data is hashed before blockchain storage
- Only verification hashes are on-chain, not PII
- GDPR "right to be forgotten" via hash invalidation
- SOC 2 Type II certified
- Full GDPR compliance documentation available
