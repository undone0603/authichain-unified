# AuthiChain Brand Kit — paste into AirOps

Use one kit. Name: **AuthiChain**. Domain: **authichain.com**.

## Foundations

**Brand name:** AuthiChain

**Domain:** authichain.com

**About the brand:**
AuthiChain is the authenticity layer for the authentic agentic economy. It lets humans and agents verify physical products, documents, and credentials with cryptographic seals, QR scans, and on-chain certificates. The mission is to authenticate global goods and stop counterfeits — positioned as U.S.-built infrastructure with public verification on authichain.com.

Sister surfaces in the same product estate:
- QRON (qron.space) — Living QR codes and artistic, scannable certificates
- StrainChain (strainchain.io) — cannabis jar / COA provenance
- GovChain (govchain.us) — contractor pursue + official document seals

Canonical product code lives in the public repo `undone0603/authichain-unified`.

**Voice and tone:**
Direct, founder-led, technical without jargon inflation. Concrete over slogans. American infrastructure framing without chest-beating. Prefer verbs: verify, seal, attest, scan, resolve. Never sound like a generic Web3 marketplace.

**Author persona:**
Founder-operator writing for procurement, compliance, brand protection, and developers who have to ship a scan that works.

**Global writing rules:**
- Customer CTAs use apex paths only: https://authichain.com/verify, https://authichain.com/dapp, https://qron.space/generate, https://govchain.us/onboard, https://strainchain.io/onboard. Never *.vercel.app.
- Do not invent customers, certifications, revenue, partners, or scan counts.
- Do not claim FedRAMP, SOC 2, HIPAA, or FDA authorization unless a public page states it as current.
- Distinguish product lines. AuthiChain is the protocol. QRON is the living QR studio. StrainChain is cannabis. GovChain is government documents.
- Prefer “verify in under a few seconds” over unsourced latency numbers. If a number is used, it must exist on a live public page.
- $QRON is a utility token discussion, not the x402 payment rail. Do not conflate them.
- Maison Élite / dropship experiments are a different company. Keep them off AuthiChain copy.
- American Seal / made-in-USA positioning is allowed. Nationalism as a substitute for product proof is not.
- No hype adjectives stacked (revolutionary, disruptive, unprecedented).
