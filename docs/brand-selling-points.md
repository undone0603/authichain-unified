## 2. StrainChain — strainchain.io
**One-liner:** Consumer-facing blockchain provenance for cannabis — METRC/BioTrack-integrated,
lab certs anchored to Bitcoin Ordinals.

**Selling points**

  stage (cultivation → processing → dispensary) as on-chain events.

## 3. GovChain — govchain.us
**One-liner:** On-chain alternative to paper-stamp government document verification.

**Selling points**
- Any municipal/agency document (permit, certificate, RFP award) gets a **Bitcoin-anchored hash**;
  the document is unchanged, the provenance becomes immutable.
- **Verification = a 2-second QR scan** — no login, no database lookup, no phone call to the agency;
  works **offline** via hash validation.
- Decentralized/tamper-evident — no central authority needed (ideal for SBIR/SVIP proposals).

**Use cases / pipeline**
- Federal agencies lose ~$2.3B/year to document fraud → direct value prop.
- Eligible: DHS SVIP Focus Area 4 (Digital Identity), DoD SBIR Topic N26-A001.
- Go-to-market: propose → pilot → statewide contract; active conversations w/ 3 Michigan agencies.

## 4. QRON — qron.space
**One-liner:** AI "living QR art" platform — QR codes become brand assets people actually want to scan.

**Selling points**
- **AI-generated QR art** (Fal.ai) — "living brand assets," ~78% higher scan rates vs plain QR.
- **Living redirects:** change a code's destination anytime — zero reprinting cost.
- **Built-in analytics:** scans by day, device breakdown, geo distribution, A/B variants.
- $QRON token (Polygon ERC-20) + staking tiers (bronze/silver/gold/platinum) for fee discounts.

**Use cases / value ladder**
- Free lead magnet (1 watermarked portal) → $5 print-ready → packs ($39 Launch / $149 Brand System)
  → subscriptions (Studio Starter $49/mo · Studio Pro $99/mo, unlimited + analytics) → Custom Labs $500+.
- Segments: restaurants, retail brands, events, agencies, cannabis (cross-sell with StrainChain).
