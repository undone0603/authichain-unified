## TruMark scope
A stamped or pictured mark, matching file hash, and QR do not prove physical platinum authenticity. A sealed-package XRF result may only represent surface or packaging-attenuated readings; state limitations explicitly. The returned report is an untrusted claim until examiner identity, permission, original report integrity and attestation authority are reviewed independently. No self-issued `ACTIVE` state.
