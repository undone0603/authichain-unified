# AuthiChain Evidence Credentials — free-first commercial scope

**Current product:** a proposed evidence credential with no hosted release and no supported billing. This document is not a purchase invitation or proof of revenue.

## Distinct customer value

- **Product owner:** signed item/batch claims linked to verifiable source records; actual item authenticity requires physical binding and evidence, never merely a QR presence.
- **MCP operator:** a reproducible snapshot of advertised service definitions and change history, with exact tool inventory and source digest. Tool execution is an entirely different test requiring separate review.
- **x402 merchant:** a snapshot of advertised payment requirements and public catalog pricing without making paid calls. Actual paid response and settlement requires a different, regulated transaction evidence workflow.
- **Agent platform:** independently verify signed receipts with pinned issuer keys; apply local trust policy that may distrust self-issued claims or require independent witnesses.

## Path to first real revenue

1. Publish offline verifier and test vectors for free; maintain read-only public evidence lookup. Provide no paid wallet prompt and no unauthorized tool calls.
2. Pilot one voluntary real customer, who supplies their own signed evidence and reviews the description. Do not treat AuthiChain's own demo product or third-party brand seed rows as customer acceptance.
3. Attach verifiable record issuance to existing **approved** Starter/DPP checkouts only when protected payment-to-fulfillment integration is released. Never create a new speculative paid issuance price, grant or subscription from this draft.
4. Require genuine, externally attributable positive-dollar checkout and Stripe signed raw webhook, stored settlement event, idempotent entitlement and completed activation. The existing founder $0/$10 test is not qualified buyer revenue.
5. Introduce paid evidence refresh, service level agreement or issuance automation only after an independent buyer has actually used the free proof and signed receipt. Use real accepted costs and measurable value, not inflated 'Trust Scores'.

## Messaging for public distribution

> AuthiChain develops portable, signed evidence credentials for product provenance and AI-agent services. A verifier can check the cryptographic issuer, subject, expiry and linked evidence digest offline; the claimed observation still requires independent corroboration. AuthiChain does not certify that a service is safe, a payment was settled, or a physical item is authentic merely because a credential is signed.

Use the term **AuthiChain Evidence Credential**, not **SealKeeper Certified** or **SealKeeper Service SEAL**. When naming SealKeeper, link its independently produced monitoring results accurately: x402 Bronze is based on unpaid challenge observation; the MCP SEAL may be suspended. The issue of third-party issuer recognition remains separate from the ability to sign bytes.

## Commercial release safety

Do not enable campaigns, `production_send`, auto-purchase, marketplace premium listings, third-party badges for backlinks or billing modifications as part of this project. Public discussions should be asynchronous and verifiable. Do not imply the issuer has an independent auditor unless one has signed a review of the exact evidence.
