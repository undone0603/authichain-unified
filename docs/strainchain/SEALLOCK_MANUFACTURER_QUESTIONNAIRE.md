# SealLock — manufacturer package-design evidence request

**Reusable technical questionnaire · Unsent · No accusation or presumption of defect**

We are evaluating first-opening integrity of flexible consumer packaging
with an apparently tear-away upper section and an internal reusable zipper.
A photographed open mouth with an intact-looking upper flap is a consumer
observation, not proof of original factory condition, intent or noncompliance.

Please answer for a **specified package design revision and production lot**,
rather than for an entire brand or general purchasing category.

## 1. Barrier geometry and access paths

1. Is a continuous, irreversible first-opening barrier applied after filling?
   If so, precisely where is it relative to the hanging hole, perforation,
   tear strip and reusable zipper?
2. Is the primary barrier a validated heat weld, destructible membrane or
   other qualified construction? Provide a cross-section/drawing with revision.
3. Can the mouth or product compartment be reached through the zipper or
   either zipper-end opening while the stated barrier remains intact?
4. Are both side seams, corners, bottom, alternative gusset paths and
   interfaces covered by the same irreversible first-opening protection?
5. If the tear-away strip is decorative or merely a hanging feature, how is
   the separately applied first-opening seal visibly distinguished?

## 2. Production controls

6. Identify the packaging operator and manufacturing process **as authorized
   for disclosure**, including lot/design revision traceability.
7. Which sealing step occurs after fill/closure, and how are validated
   temperature, pressure and dwell settings recorded?
8. Which in-line inspections, destructive seal samples, calibration checks,
   reject/quarantine rules and rework controls verify correct production?
9. What documented corrective action exists for absent/weak seals or
   accessible zippers prior to first irreversible opening?

## 3. Independent challenge and methods

10. Has a pre-registered, continuous first-opening bypass challenge been run
    on as-received, untouched units across zipper ends and all side/gusset routes?
    Provide sample plan, accepted failure criterion and first-opening video.
11. Which material-specific seal-strength or leak methods apply? Examples
    include ASTM F88/F88M-23 and, if technically appropriate, F3039-23.
    State why each method fits the design and what it does not establish.
12. Supply the predeclared acceptance thresholds, results, uncertainties,
    applicable sample counts, test dates, independent reviewer identity and
    original report hashes. A self-asserted "passed" field is not certification.

## 4. Retail and traceability

13. What inspection does the receiving retailer perform before accepting or
    selling stock? How are intact first-opening barriers recorded?
14. How are specific lots connected to approved design revisions and
    inspection evidence, and how are failures/corrective actions traced?
15. If issuer signing is offered, what trust anchor or public key permits
    independent verification of the signer's actual authorization?

## Confidentiality and decision boundaries

Do not submit unredacted customer data, cannabis products, personal
identifiers, payment credentials, trade secrets or private documents through
public GitHub issues or public forms. Exchange controlled documents through
an agreed secure channel with an authorized reviewer. Do not ship restricted
materials without separately establishing lawful handling.

A complete reply makes the evidence **ready for independent technical review**,
not automatically an authentic package, a compliant sale or a certified seal.
The StrainChain software remains read-only and release-held. Neither a copied
barcode nor a SHA-256 digest proves issuer authority, time of packaging or
physical seal integrity.
