# Shareable verification evidence — opt-in integration copy

Status: documentation template, **not** an issued certificate, independent endorsement, customer testimonial, or evidence of paid adoption. Owner: AuthiChain adoption issue [#2487](https://github.com/undone0603/authichain-unified/issues/2487).

## Copy/paste README example (only on an owned repository or with explicit publisher consent)

> **Verify, don't assume.** AuthiChain provides a way to inspect a product or record's available verification evidence. A result is not a regulatory certification or a guarantee that an item is genuine. Open the official [AuthiChain verification page](https://authichain.com/verify), review the actual status, issuer, provenance and timestamp when available, and independently re-check the underlying record. Unknown, not-found, unverified, and revoked results are **not** successful verification.

Do not embed a real serial number, buyer identity, private provenance record, or a screenshot of another party's evidence without permission. Do not turn a demo or simulation into a verified-result badge.

## Evidence-sharing acceptance checklist

- Obtain the record owner's permission to publish the exact evidence fields; otherwise share only a generic link to the verification service.
- Preserve the exact original result semantics. **Verified** means the stated cryptographic or record checks passed within their defined scope, **not** that a regulator certified a product. **Not found**, **unknown**, **unverified**, and **revoked** must remain distinct.
- Include the original issuer and observed timestamp only when actually returned by the verification service; do not fabricate issuer, time, cryptographic signature, or result.
- Prefer a public independently re-verifiable URL over a static screenshot. Redact private identifiers and tokens. Do not publish credentials, private serials, customer emails, or paid receipts.
- Label examples and simulated records as simulations. Never imply an external organization integrated, approved, or paid without independent evidence.
- For an agent integration: discover the free verifier first; request explicit buyer authorization before following any paid x402 challenge. A 402 response is an offer, not a settlement receipt.

## Attribution and conversion measurement (privacy-preserving)

Count separate milestones: **public discovery → free independent verification → explicit integrator opt-in → independently attributable third-party usage → authorized settled payment → fulfilled entitlement**. Track source, observation date, consent, and verification method. Exclude bots, founders/house accounts, free or zero-dollar tests, unfulfilled payment intents, and refunded/disputed payments from external paid conversion. Do not infer distinct humans from wallet addresses or page views.

Only append campaign parameters to owned links where permitted; never include user IDs, email addresses, serials, or secrets in URL parameters. Never automatically send outreach or request payment.

This page is a proposed documentation aid only. It does not change verification verdicts, checkout, credentials, deployed routes, pricing, or production permissions. Publication and any real customer case study require source review and consent.
