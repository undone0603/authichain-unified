## 4. Key Objections & Rebuttals
*   **Objection:** "Why not just a normal QR code?"
*   **Rebuttal:** Normal QRs are easily faked/replaced. QRON is cryptographically signed and tied to an immutable record, preventing "man-in-the-middle" physical attacks.
*   **Objection:** "Blockchain is too complex."
*   **Rebuttal:** The user never sees the blockchain. They see a cinematic "StoryMode" reveal. The blockchain is the silent insurance policy.
