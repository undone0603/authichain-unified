AgentZ · Voice + Vision

Speak to AgentZ while showing a photo, camera frame, or screen. AgentZ observes and suggests; it never clicks, deploys or sends on your behalf.

Microphone transcription is handled by your browser. Depending on the browser, speech recognition may use its provider's service. Typing avoids this.

The camera/screen is accessed only when you click Capture, with browser permission. A single resized still image is sent to your configured private-network model; no continuous stream is uploaded.
